## **3. Short Description of Ask**

Diageo is seeking a scalable MVP version of “Smart Shelf,” a GenAI application to enrich product descriptions, optimize search rank, reduce non-compliance, and drive productivity across ecommerce retailers (Walmart, Kroger, Amazon, Instacart).



## **5. Functional Requirements (From RFI)**

### **Product & UX**

* Stand-alone Azure-based app; simple task-based UI.
* No prompt engineering required.
* Seasonal/trend supplemental instructions.

### **Data & Integration**

* Full integration with **Stibo MDM** (source of truth).
* Pull product data → enrich in Smart Shelf → push back to Stibo.
* Stibo workflows remain unchanged.

### **AI & Content**

* LLM-enriched descriptions optimized for retailer rules (Walmart, Kroger, Amazon).
* Bulk + individual SKU workflows.
* Built inside Diageo’s Azure OpenAI Sandbox.
* Retailer compliance + keyword-enrichment built in.

### **Testing**

* Required in-market test across a limited number of retailers.
* Must deliver statistically relevant results. 

---

## **6. Assumptions & Constraints (From RFI)**

* MVP scope may change due to budget constraints.
* Keyword generation limited to modifying approved keywords.
* Stibo API access assumed.
* All data must live inside Diageo’s Azure environment.
* Diageo to procure keyword search-volume tool license (e.g., Keyword Planner).
