## 2) HEAD-TO-HEAD BATTLE CARDS

### ACCENTURE INTERACTIVE

**Core Strengths (This Scope)**
- Enterprise governance + risk management credibility (appeals to Sarah McLeod, legal/regulatory anxieties)
- Proven large-scale change management and stakeholder alignment playbooks
- Deep retailer relationships and category experience (spirits/beverages context awareness)
- Global scale (relevant if Diageo expands beyond North America)
- Integrated strategy + delivery reduces handoff risk

**Recurring Weaknesses (This Scope)**
- Long sales cycles + slow time-to-value (MVP timeline risk; Diageo wants Q2/Q3 2025 validation)
- Heavy team overhead inflates cost; difficult to justify for MVP-stage spend (budget constraint landmine)
- Product innovation speed lags pure-play SaaS/GenAI vendors
- Accenture's GenAI maturity largely services-driven, not product-native
- Tendency toward gold-plating scope; scope creep on integration complexity

**Likely Narratives / Angles They Will Run**
- "End-to-end digital transformation roadmap; we've done this at scale for beverage leaders"
- "Risk mitigation through proven governance and compliance frameworks"
- "Single partner reduces vendor fragmentation; we own the full stack"
- "Our change management rigor ensures adoption and sustained ROI"

**Discovery Prompts That Expose Gaps (≥3)**
1. *"Walk us through your last three product enrichment / GenAI launches—how long from kickoff to live market?"* → Expect 6–9 month responses (vs. Bounteous' proven POC → MVP trajectory)
2. *"What is your typical team composition for a 10K–50K SKU optimization sprint, and what is monthly burn?"* → Will likely exceed Diageo's MVP budget envelope
3. *"Do you have a standalone GenAI content product, or are we building custom?"* → Likely custom build = longer timeline, higher cost
4. *"How do you isolate and validate search rank lift without confounding variables in a 12-week pilot?"* → May propose extensive control group / statistical design (good, but time-intensive)

**Counters to Common Claims**
| Accenture Claim | Our Counter | Proof / Citation |
|---|---|---|
| "We have proven retail transformation experience" | ✓ True, but retail ops ≠ product description GenAI | We have a **live POC** with Diageo that proved search rank + compliance gains; ready to scale now |
| "Our governance reduces risk" | Governance ≠ speed; Smart Shelf compliance is baked into the model, not added overhead | Opportunity Brief: "Sarah McLeod anxiety = risky claims"; our guardrails (zero hallucination, legal-tier controls) are native to product, not process layer |
| "We'll own the full stack" | Full-stack integration often means longer time-to-value and cost overruns on integration | Diageo requirement (Daniel Rodriguez): "clean integration with Stibo, no disruptions"; our lightweight API approach vs. their likely heavy ecosystem play |

**Proof Moments We Should Emphasize**
- **Bounteous POC success**: Prior engagement de-risks this opportunity; continuity advantage is real (Opportunity Brief: "strategic insight and continuity advantage which competitors may lack")
- **Search rank gains**: Case study of POC search rank improvements (Off-Deck Play #1, 2-week prep)
- **Speed**: MVP structure with phased rollout vs. long-cycle transformation engagement
- **Compliance rigor**: Compliance testing frameworks validated with Sarah McLeod pre-proposal (Risk Register: "Pre-market compliance reviews")

**Watch-Outs**
- Accenture may offer a "follow-the-sun" global delivery model at Diageo's global ambitions; counter with: *"MVP North America first; we scale faster than you staff."*
- They will emphasize their Walmart/Kroger/Amazon relationships; ask: *"Who on your team owns retail compliance requirements, not just account management?"*
- Risk of them bundling compliance review into a larger governance engagement (cost scope creep); insist on decoupling MVP scope.
