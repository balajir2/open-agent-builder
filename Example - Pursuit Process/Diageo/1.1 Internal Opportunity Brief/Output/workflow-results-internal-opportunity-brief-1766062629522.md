Although ecommerce currently constitutes a small share of Diageo's sales, its potential for growth—both in North America and globally—is pivotal for driving both online awareness and offline sales.

## Our Angle of Attack

Our proposal leverages a GenAI-powered application, Smart Shelf, to enrich product descriptions and optimize search rank. This directly aligns with Diageo's need to improve visibility, discoverability, and compliance across online marketplaces. By employing Generative AI, we aim to reduce the time and cost associated with creating retailer-optimized descriptions and minimize compliance risks.

Our differentiated approach, validated through the POC with Bounteous, is timely as it not only addresses Diageo's KPIs like search rank and sales improvement but also supports marketers with an easy-to-use, low-risk tool. The MVP initiative is their opportunity to validate the efficacy of the GenAI solution and unlock broader business impact.
